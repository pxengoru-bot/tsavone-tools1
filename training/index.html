    How we deliver

    Five ways to learn with us

    We choose the format that fits your people, budget and context, from a classroom in Kampala to a demonstration plot in the field.

    Open enrolment

    Scheduled courses in Kampala and Gulu. Join professionals from other organisations and learn from their experience.

    12–25 participants

    In-house

    Delivered at your premises for your team, using your own projects, data and documents as working examples.

    10–30 participants

    Training of trainers

    Build a pool of trainers who cascade skills to staff, farmers or communities, with manuals and session plans.

    15–25 trainers

    Blended

    Live online sessions plus one or two in-person days. Less time away from work, with the practice that matters done face to face.

    15–40 participants

    Field-based

    Learning on farms, wetlands, settlements and project sites, where skills are practised in the real setting.

    10–25 participants

    What every participant receives

    • A course workbook to work through during and after the course
    • Templates and checklists from the Tsavone toolkit, ready to use at work
    • A personal action plan for applying what was learned
    • A certificate of completion

    How a course runs

    1. 1
      Needs check

      We find out what participants already know and what they need to do differently.

    2. 2
      Tailored design

      Examples, case studies and exercises are adapted to your sector and context.

    3. 3
      Active delivery

      Short inputs, then practice: group work, real documents, field visits and role plays.

    4. 4
      Assessment

      Practical exercises and before-and-after checks show what has been learned.

    5. 5
      Completion report

      Sponsors receive a short report on attendance, results and recommendations.

    6. 6
      Follow-up

      We check in on action plans and offer support as skills are put to work.
